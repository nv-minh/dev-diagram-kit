# US-003 — Every transition is recorded and readable as history

**As an** auditor, **I want** every workflow transition recorded with actor, timestamp, and prior state, and readable from the claim, **so that** I reconstruct decisions from the system instead of email threads.

## Context

The audit-trail slice: write on every transition (all stories route through it) + the read surface on claim detail and history search.

## Linked Requirements

[[docs/atlas-re/srs/atlas-re-spec.md#FR-atlas-re-002|FR-atlas-re-002]] · [[docs/atlas-re/srs/atlas-re-spec.md#FR-atlas-re-003|FR-atlas-re-003]] · NFR-atlas-re-002

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-001: Given a claim moves FILED → VALIDATED, when the transition commits, then a history row records the analyst, the timestamp, and FILED as prior state.
- AC-002: Given a claim with four past transitions, when a user opens the claim detail, then all four rows show in order.
- AC-003: Given any database role used by the application, when it attempts UPDATE or DELETE on the history table, then the operation is denied (NFR-atlas-re-002).
