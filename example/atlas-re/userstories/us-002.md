# US-002 — Handler works a state-ordered claim queue

**As a** claims handler, **I want** a queue of claims ordered by how long they've sat in their state, showing whose action each is waiting on, **so that** nothing stalls silently in my spreadsheet.

## Context

The daily work surface — the queue every other story routes into. Replaces the personal-spreadsheet tracking named in the URD.

## Linked Requirements

[[docs/atlas-re/srs/atlas-re-spec.md#FR-atlas-re-001|FR-atlas-re-001]]

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-001: Given three open claims aged 5, 2, and 9 days in their current states, when the handler opens the workspace, then the queue lists them 9-5-2.
- AC-002: Given a claim waiting on Finance validation, when it appears in the queue, then its row shows "waiting on Finance".
