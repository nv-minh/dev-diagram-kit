# US-001 — Approver decides a claim within their tier

**As an** approver, **I want** validated claims routed to me only when the amount is within my authority, **so that** small claims stop waiting on the Head of Claims.

## Context

The tier-routing slice of the approval capability. The queue surface itself is US-002; this story is the routing rule and the decision act.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-001: Given a VALIDATED claim of 40,000 USD, when tier routing runs, then it lands in a handler-tier queue.
- AC-002: Given a VALIDATED claim of exactly 50,000 USD, when tier routing runs, then it lands in a handler-tier queue (boundary: ≤ 50k).
- AC-003: Given a VALIDATED claim of 50,001 USD, when tier routing runs, then it lands in a manager-tier queue (boundary: just above 50k).
- AC-004: Given a VALIDATED claim of 300,000 USD, when tier routing runs, then it lands in the committee queue.
- AC-005: Given the approver also validated this claim, when they submit a decision, then the system blocks it and names the validator (E-atlas-re-001).
- AC-006: Given no FX rate exists for the filing date, when tier routing runs, then the claim is flagged "tier pending" and not routed (E-atlas-re-002).
