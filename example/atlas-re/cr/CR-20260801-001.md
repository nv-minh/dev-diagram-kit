# CR-20260801-001 — Raise the handler authority threshold to 60k

> Change: handler-tier ceiling 50,000 → 60,000 USD · Recorded 2026-08-01 · Status: draft (not yet applied)

## 1. Rationale

Finance review of Q2 claim volumes showed handler-tier claims cluster just under 50k (the current ceiling), and the resulting manager-tier queue is the new bottleneck. Raising the handler ceiling to 60k moves ~15% of volume back to the handler tier without changing the committee threshold (risk appetite unchanged above 250k).

## 3. Detailed Impact

The change is a single-threshold move, scoped to the handler↔manager boundary. The committee tier (≤ 250k / above) is untouched, so high-value risk controls are unaffected. Every artifact that pins the 50k number must move to 60k in dependency order: spec (the BR + FR) → the story ACs → the checklist row → the test-case boundary values. The wireframe `[3] Decision panel` shows the tier label generically ("your tier: manager") so it needs no edit.
